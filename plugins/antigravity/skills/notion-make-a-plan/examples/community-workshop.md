# Plan a community printmaking workshop

This fictional example shows how a brief becomes a connected event plan. Adapt
its structure and visual choices to the actual event.

## Starting brief

> Plan our community printmaking workshop for December 12, 2026, from 14:00 to
> 17:00 in New York. We expect 24 guests and have a $900 budget. Maya coordinates
> the event and Jules handles materials. The studio is holding the room until
> November 20 but has not confirmed the booking or price. Jules has a $180
> materials estimate. We still need a refreshments quote.
>
> Maya needs to confirm the room by November 20. Jules should prepare the
> materials list by November 23. Invitations can go out only after the room is
> confirmed. Please draft the invitation and a day-of run sheet. Don't send them.
> RSVP deadline is December 5. We have no guest names yet. Use a warm, welcoming
> style with terracotta and cream. Create the plan privately.

## Connected pages

**🎨 Community printmaking workshop** is a private home with **Preparations** and
**Guests** child databases and a **Run sheet** page. Preparations contains the
working invitation record; the home links to that record rather than copying its
text. Guests starts empty because no names were supplied.

The home uses a shallow cream cover with original terracotta linework, a palette
icon, and a narrow left action column beside a wider preparations table. The
left column shows the next action and compact links to Invitation, Run sheet,
and Guests. The right begins with upcoming preparations, with full titles,
status, owner labels, and due dates. A lightly tinted **Next: confirm the room**
callout names the November 20 hold deadline. The event date, time, location and
budget sit just below the title. Long resource notes remain in the relevant rows.

The budget summary reads: **$900 budget · $180 estimated materials · room and
refreshments costs unknown.** It does not claim $720 remains available, because
other costs are unpriced. The event is planned, not confirmed.

These fictional names are text labels, not guessed Notion identities. Unknown
owners and dates remain empty properties. The table includes proposed organizing
tasks derived from the brief; it does not invent deadlines for them. All rows
relate to the plan. A **Needs a date** view retains the three undated preparations.
A **Calendar** view shows the three dated tasks; its dates are preparation deadlines,
not the event's start time. A **Completed** view retains finished work later.

## Inside “Confirm studio booking and price”

**Next action:** Maya to request written confirmation and the room price before
the November 20 hold expires.

**Complete when:** the booking is confirmed, the price and payment terms are
recorded from the studio's response, and the invitation has the confirmed venue.

**Why it matters:** invitations depend on a confirmed room. If the room cannot
be confirmed, propose a different venue or date before sending anything.

**Related work:** Review workshop invitation. A native dependency relation points
to this booking task. A Plan relation returns to the home.

## Inside “Review workshop invitation”

**Draft — venue confirmation needed**

**Subject:** Spend an afternoon making prints with us

Join us for a community printmaking workshop on December 12, from 2–5 p.m.
New York time. We'll spend the afternoon exploring printmaking together.

We're planning for 24 guests and finalizing the studio booking. Please let us
know by December 5 if you'd like to join; we'll share the confirmed venue details
before the event.

**Before sending:** confirm the venue, add the actual RSVP destination, and check
whether participants need to bring materials. Those details were not supplied.
The draft is saved in the invitation record and has not been sent.

## Run sheet

Label this as a **proposed schedule**, since the brief supplies only the overall
event time:

| New York local time | Activity                 | Preparation                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 14:00–14:15         | Welcome and introduction | Confirm the facilitator and materials setup            |
| 14:15–14:45         | Demonstration            | Jules to advise what materials the demonstration needs |
| 14:45–16:30         | Printmaking time         | Arrange work surfaces and supplies                     |
| 16:30–17:00         | Share work and clean up  | Confirm cleanup responsibilities                       |

Do not assign a facilitator or describe supplies as purchased. Link the materials
task and the event home. The Guests database has Name, RSVP, Plus-ones, and Contact
fields for later entry; no guests are marked invited and no public form is created.

## Handoff

> Your workshop plan is ready, with preparations, an invitation draft, and a
> proposed run sheet. Start with Confirm studio booking and price—the room hold
> expires November 20. Who will facilitate, and who should handle refreshments?

The handoff includes the actual home link. Those answers refine the existing
records after the first useful version is delivered.
