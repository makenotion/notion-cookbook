# Use cases

The structure is always the same: a plan page, a database and views. Each section below only changes the context: what to look for, what a row is, the work to draft, and the additions likely to help. Use the "Look for" lists to spot what's missing, not as a questionnaire.

## Event (offsite, workshop, party, wedding)

- **Look for:** date and time with timezone, guest count, budget, hosts, and what's already confirmed.
- **Rows:** preparations. Book the long-lead items first (venue, food, speakers), then invitations, final numbers and a day-of run sheet. Keep booking deadlines separate from the event date.
- **Draft:** the invitation, the run sheet, and a vendor shortlist only from real sources.
- **Useful additions:** RSVP form and guest list, calendar view of deadlines, share with co-hosts, invites and signs (Canva for finished designs).
- **Watch for:** keep estimated and confirmed costs separate, keep the guest list private, and invite no one without a yes.

## Launch (product, feature, store opening)

- **Look for:** what goes live, for whom, the date (target or committed), and owners for each workstream.
- **Rows:** deliverables by workstream (product, marketing, support, sales, measurement), ending in a go/no-go check before launch. Link engineering work to its existing plan instead of copying it.
- **Draft:** the announcement, the help article and the launch-day checklist.
- **Useful additions:** timeline, progress chart, kickoff on the calendar with a linked meeting note, landing page (a Notion Site, or Vercel for a custom site), visuals for the announcement.
- **Watch for:** targets aren't results. Keep a results section for real numbers after launch.

## Marketing campaign

- **Look for:** audience, the one message, offer, channels and the launch date.
- **Rows:** assets, one per asset and channel. Steps: draft → review → final → published. Several campaigns over time call for a Campaigns database related to content.
- **Draft:** copy and scripts inside each asset's page, marked as drafts.
- **Useful additions:** calendar view, draft-to-live board, visuals (concepts first, then finished assets in Canva or the person's design tool), comments for review.
- **Watch for:** nothing gets published or sent by creating the plan.

## Content calendar ("a month of posts," "I need to post more")

- **Look for:** channels, a realistic posting rhythm, 3–5 themes, the voice.
- **Rows:** posts (Idea → Drafting → Ready → Published) with channel, publish date, caption and a visual idea. On later runs, add to the same calendar and never rebuild it.
- **Draft:** the captions, written in the rows.
- **Useful additions:** calendar view plus a "Needs a date" view, keep it moving (a weekly check that fills the next posts), visuals (concepts, then finished posts in a matching style).
- **Watch for:** forms, sites and meetings rarely fit. A visual idea isn't a finished asset.

## Trip

- **Look for:** the chosen destination (choosing one is research), dates, travelers, budget, bookings.
- **Rows:** itinerary items (To book → Booked → Done) with local time, place, confirmation and cost. Keep preparation tasks separate from timed items.
- **Draft:** a packing and documents checklist.
- **Useful additions:** calendar view, share with fellow travelers. Usually no outside tools.
- **Watch for:** only add up known costs, never mix currencies without a stated rate, and treat an estimate as an estimate.

## Job search

- **Look for:** target roles, location or remote, and the date to aim for.
- **Rows:** applications (Interested → Applied → Interviewing → Offer / Closed) with company, role, source, next action and follow-up date.
- **Draft:** interview prep pages and a story bank, built only from real achievements.
- **Useful additions:** board by stage, calendar of interviews and follow-ups, follow-up reminders, a designed résumé (Canva) only if they ask about how it looks.
- **Watch for:** creating a draft doesn't advance an application.

## Team project (ongoing project management)

- **Look for:** the outcome, scope, the team and its roles, the cadence, and existing Projects, Tasks or Docs.
- **Rows:** tasks in the shared Tasks database related to the project (Notion Projects). Record decisions and blockers with source, impact and next action.
- **Draft:** the project brief and the first meeting agenda.
- **Useful additions:** timeline, "My tasks" view for each person, keep it moving (a scheduled weekly check-in), a check-in meeting on the calendar with a linked note, share and assign teammates.
- **Watch for:** if another system owns the tasks (Linear, Jira), link to it rather than building a competing tracker.

## Move or other personal goal

- **Look for:** what done looks like, the date, and who's involved.
- **Rows:** a checklist for a short plan, or dated tasks with dependencies for a bigger one (keys before the movers, a confirmed date before booking).
- **Draft:** packing, handoff and purchase lists.
- **Useful additions:** reminders, calendar view.
- **Watch for:** keep quotes, reservations and paid expenses separate.
